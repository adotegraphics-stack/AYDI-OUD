Sustainable Agroforestry & Ethical Cultivation

140,000+ Trees. Generations of Green Wealth.

AYDI operates India's premier network of sustainable Aquilaria plantations. Located across fertile mist-laden hills, our agroforestry model integrates cutting-edge silvicultural research with grassroots farming cooperatives — ensuring steady natural resin yield, environmental conservation, and generational prosperity for local communities.

140,000+ Aquilaria Trees Under Stewardship
12+ Years Silvicultural Dedication & Research
100% Ethical Cooperative Farming Model
Zero Wild Depletion Certified Sustainable Regeneration
Terraced Mountain Agroforestry

Hillside Agroforestry & High-Altitude Groves

Sprawling plantations established across scenic mountainous elevations, offering optimal soil drainage, abundant rainfall, and cool mist essential for vigorous Aquilaria growth.

Community Stewardship

Grassroots Farmer Partnerships

Partnering with indigenous farming communities with fair-trade agreements, technical guidance, and sapling provision to cultivate generational green wealth.

Scientific Forestry

Scientific Canopy Audits & Inoculation

Certified AYDI forestry professionals monitor tree vigor, trunk girth, and organic bio-induction to ensure optimal, natural resin defense development.

Hand-Tended Nurturing

Nurturing from Nursery to Canopy

Every sapling is selected from verified parent seed trees and hand-planted into rich natural humus, nurtured without synthetic pesticides.

Monitored Maturity

Mature Grove Life-Cycle Management

Continuous field audits allow our botanists to determine the exact maturity window when heartwood resin concentration reaches peak aromatic perfection.

Sustainable Harvest

Reverent & Ecological Harvesting

Harvesting follows strict zero-waste protocols. Resinous heartwood is carefully preserved, while surrounding sapwood is repurposed for natural incense and organic distillation.

About AYDI

Building a sustainable Agarwood industry.

AYDI Agarwood Pvt. Ltd. is an India-based enterprise pioneering the complete lifecycle of premium Agarwood (Oud) — uniting sustainable forestry, advanced processing, fine jewelry manufacturing, and global trade under one roof.

Guided by environmental integrity and respect for tradition, AYDI brings transparency, consistent resin quality, and design innovation to one of the world's most precious botanical resources.

Our Heritage

From a Vision to a Generational Legacy.

The story of AYDI began with a conviction: that the extraordinary potential of Indian Agarwood deserved to be developed with scientific rigor, environmental stewardship, and timeless artistic design.

Founded by Mr. Mohammed Shanir Malikulam, AYDI’s journey unites years of field research, silvicultural dedication, and trusted partnerships with traditional farming communities across India.

The Vision Recognizing the enduring global value and cultural resonance of Indian Oud.
First Plantations Establishing initial cultivation sites and sustainable cooperative models for local growers.
Modernization Developing proprietary processing methods and artisanal manufacturing capabilities.
Today & Beyond A vertically integrated luxury brand with over 140,000 trees and worldwide reach.
